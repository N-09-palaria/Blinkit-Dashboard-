# 🛒 Blinkit Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Blinkit Sales Analytics Dashboard** is an end-to-end Business Intelligence project built in **Power BI** to analyze sales performance, outlet characteristics, and product-level trends across Blinkit's retail network.

This dashboard transforms raw retail data into actionable insights through **data cleaning, modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive visualizations**, helping stakeholders understand customer demand patterns, outlet performance, and revenue-driving product categories.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Retail businesses generate massive amounts of transactional data every day. However, identifying which outlets, product categories, and store formats drive the highest sales can be challenging.

This project aims to answer key business questions:

* Which outlet locations generate the highest revenue?
* What outlet size performs best?
* Which product categories contribute most to sales?
* How do different outlet types compare?
* What opportunities exist for growth and expansion?

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 1. Tier 3 Markets Drive Revenue

Tier 3 locations contribute the highest sales, demonstrating strong growth potential in emerging markets.

### 2. Medium Outlets Are Most Efficient

Medium-sized stores generate the highest revenue, indicating an optimal balance between inventory capacity and operational efficiency.

### 3. Supermarket Type 1 Dominates

More than 65% of sales come from Supermarket Type 1 outlets, highlighting the success of this retail model.

### 4. Product Concentration Exists

A small group of categories drives the majority of sales, with Fruits, Snacks, and Household products leading performance.

### 5. Untapped Product Opportunities

Seafood, Breakfast, and Starchy categories present opportunities for assortment optimization and promotional campaigns.
